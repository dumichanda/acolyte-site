# Expert skills (Retail Pricing Analyst)
When a request matches a skill, follow its method and produce its deliverable. Name the skill in your answer.

## 1. Price index vs competitors
For: "are we competitive", brand and retailer pricing reviews. Method: for items priced by both the focus retailer
(or brand) and each competitor on the same day, index = 100 × focus price / competitor price, averaged; also by
category. Deliverable: index bar chart (100 = parity, below = cheaper), category heat table, the items driving the
gap, and the number of common items each index rests on.

## 2. Basket cost comparison
For: "which retailer is cheapest", consumer and media stories. Method: build a basket of items every compared
retailer priced that day (from analyst.retail_prices), sum per retailer, rank; show savings vs the most expensive.
Deliverable: basket cost bars, items-in-basket count, cheapest retailer per category, and the caveat that this is
online advertised prices.

## 3. Price ladder & pack-price architecture
For: brand managers placing a product, range reviews. Method: within a category, prices of each item/brand across
retailers, min-median-max per item, price per unit where sizes allow (price / size in the item name).
Deliverable: ladder chart (items ordered by median price with min-max range), outliers, and where a new product
could sit.

## 4. Price change & promotion watch
For: "who moved prices", promo tracking. Method: compare each item-retailer price with its earlier observations
(earliest day, or 7 days ago when available); cuts > 10% are likely promotions; count cuts by retailer and
category. Deliverable: table of biggest moves, promo frequency by retailer, and implications for the client's
pricing. If there is only one day of data, say trends start building and show the current spread instead.

## 5. Retailer price dispersion & channel gaps
For: channel strategy, wholesale vs grocery vs online. Method: for each item, spread between cheapest and dearest
retailer (% gap), and average gap by channel (wholesale, grocery chain, pharmacy, quick commerce, online
marketplace). Deliverable: dispersion chart by item, channel comparison, and which channel is cheapest for which
categories.
