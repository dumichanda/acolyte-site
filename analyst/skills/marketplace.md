# Expert skills (Marketplace Analyst: Amazon.co.za and Takealot)
When a request matches a skill, follow its method and produce its deliverable. Name the skill in your answer.
Focus on the 'online marketplace' channel (retailer 'Amazon.co.za' and 'Takealot'), compared with the rest.

## 1. Amazon.co.za vs Takealot head-to-head
Method: items both priced the same day; who is cheaper on how many items, average gap %, by category.
Deliverable: win/loss count, gap chart by category, the items with the biggest gaps.

## 2. Marketplace vs bricks-and-mortar price gap
Method: each marketplace price vs the cheapest grocery chain or wholesale price for the same item and day.
Deliverable: index vs grocery and wholesale (100 = parity), categories where marketplaces win or lose.

## 3. Assortment & coverage
Method: which basket items each marketplace lists, how many sellers/offers (retail_offers), ratings and review counts.
Deliverable: coverage table, gaps (items not listed), review-count leaders as a proxy for popularity.

## 4. Third-party seller pricing
Method: retail_offers where retailer is Amazon.co.za or Takealot, seller variants (e.g. 'Amazon.co.za - Seller'),
spread between sellers for the same item. Deliverable: seller spread table and where marketplace sellers overprice.

## 5. Marketplace price moves
Method: price changes over time for marketplace offers versus grocery chains; frequency and size of cuts.
Deliverable: biggest moves, which marketplace reprices more often, implications for brands selling there.

## Expert models and plain-language glossary (use these words when explaining)
- Price position index: a seller's price ÷ the market median for the same product × 100. Above 100 = dearer than most.
- Buy Box / featured offer: the default seller a shopper buys from. Price, stock and rating decide who wins it.
- Review velocity: new reviews per week. A proxy for sales momentum; ratings without volume mean little.
- Rating gap: the difference to the category average rating. Half a star moves conversion noticeably.
- Price dispersion across sellers: wide spread = shoppers comparing and sellers competing on price.
- Seasonality: Black Friday (late November) and month-end move both prices and demand. Compare like with like.
